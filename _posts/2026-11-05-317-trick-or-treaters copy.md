Halloween was on a Saturday and it was nice and clear.

We forgot we were starting at 5:30 and we were running late anyway, trying to eat dinner, so we didn't start until 6:00. We did have a few doorbells between 5:30 and 6:00, but not too many, so I can't imagine we really missed a lot. It did seem like 5:30 was pretty early to get started.

Missing/incongruous data:

- 2017 we were remodeling the house and didn't hand out candy.
- 2018 I walked with Phoenix and the tally got lost somewhere.
- 2020 was COVID and no one handed out candy.
- 2022 we were getting our floors redone due to a leak and the whole house was torn up so we didn't hand out candy.

Our costumes this year:

- Me: Ghostbuster
- Jenn: Joker
- Phoenix: (Not pictured) Coraline

![Jenn as Joker, me as a Ghostbuster]({{ site.url }}/images/20261105_costumes.jpg)

I've wanted to be a Ghostbuster since I can remember. When the HasLabs proton pack and wand came out, I ordered it and I've been planning this for almost a year.

I didn't sew any of the stuff this time. Since COVID, apparel fabric shops are scarce. Plus, for a flight/jumpsuit like this, the amount of time and materials to make it vs. build it doesn't make sense. Vastly cheaper to buy it.

I did modify the pack to have a larger speaker so it sounds better. I also added some aftermarket stickers to it that stick far better and look more realistic than the stock stickers.

![Me as a Ghostbuster]({{ site.url }}/images/20261105_ghostbusters1.jpg)

What's cool is that it also goes with our outdoor decor since we have a Stay-Puft Marshmallow Man and Slimer on our front porch.

![It's the Stay-Puft Marshmallow Man]({{ site.url }}/images/20261105_ghostbusters2.jpg)

I would occasionally go outside, "power up" the pack (which makes all the noises like it's spinning up), and "shoot" the proton wand at the kids up and down the street. This, admittedly, seemed to draw them in as some would cross the street to come over after this. I can't say this _didn't_ contribute to the increased number of trick-or-treaters. I had one trick-or-treater ask if they could get a picture of me by Stay-Puft and I was happy to oblige.

Parents also appreciated the costume and we found several groups singing the Ghostbusters theme song or yelling, "WHO YOU GONNA CALL?" from the sidewalk.

I will likely reuse this _many_ times over coming years since it's an easy costume to get into, it's reasonably comfortable, and it's a big hit with the kids.

This year was also, unfortunately, the last year for the annual Halloween party at our good friends' house that we've gone to for... well, this is 26 years, I think.

I enjoy sewing and making costumes, for sure. It's gotten a lot harder with the lack of fabric stores, especially now that Jo-Ann is gone. Lots of _quilting_ fabric, very little _apparel_ fabric. I was making a new costume every year to start so I could be something different each year at that Halloween party and at work in the office. After that, it was also so I could go walking around with Phoenix.

My kiddo is getting too old to walk around the neighborhood. COVID shut down my office, and I think contributed to the loss of Jo-Ann. Now that the Halloween party is over... it's just me.

Which won't stop me from making things, of course, but it just means I can be more thoughtful about what I want to make and also reuse the costumes I make instead of going for something new every year.

But, dammit, I'll sure miss those parties.
